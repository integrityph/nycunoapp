Registration guide

How to upload your Social Security Number

Having trouble with the Social Security step in the app? Follow the steps below — each numbered marker on the screenshots shows exactly what to tap or fill in.

  1. 1

    Go to the Document section

    In the registration app, open the Document section. You'll see a card labeled Social Security...

  2. 2

    Tap Social Security and fill in the details

    Tap the Social Security card to open the form, then follow the numbered markers below in order.

    1. 1 Tap this card to open the Social Security form.
    2. 2 Tap here to upload a clear photo of yourself — this is a photo of you, not of a document.
    3. 3 Type your 9-digit Social Security Number here.
    4. 4 Tap Save once everything looks correct.
  3. 3

    Find your Social Security Number

    Not sure where to look? Your number is printed on your Social Security card, as shown below. Marker 3 matches the same number from the form in step 2.

    Sample Social Security card — the 9-digit number is near the top

  4. 4

    Save

    Once your photo and number are both entered, tap Save (marker 4 in step 2). Your Social Security step is now complete.

Before you save

  • •Use a clear, recent photo of yourself — good lighting, no sunglasses or hats, and your face fully visible.
  • •Double-check your 9-digit Social Security Number before saving — it must be entered correctly for your tax forms to process.
  • •Your Social Security Number is sensitive personal information — only enter it on a private, secure connection.

Still having trouble? Contact support from within the app for further assistance.
