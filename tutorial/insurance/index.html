How to upload your FHV Insurance Certificate

Having trouble with the FHV Insurance Certificate step in the app? Follow the steps below — each numbered marker on the screenshots shows exactly what to tap or fill in.

  1. 1

    Go to the Vehicles section

    In the registration app, open the Vehicles section and scroll down to FHV Insurance Certificate.

  2. 2

    Fill in the FHV Insurance Certificate details

    Follow the numbered markers below in order to complete this section.

    1. 1 Tap here to upload a clear photo of your FHV insurance certificate.
    2. 2 Type your Policy Number here — it goes in the Document Number field.
    3. 3 Select the expiration date shown on your certificate.
    4. 4 Tap Save once everything looks correct.
  3. 3

    Find the details on your insurance certificate

    Not sure where to look? Markers 2 and 3 below match the same numbers from the form in step 2.

    1. 2 Policy Number — type this into the Document Number field.
    2. 3 Expiration Date — select this same date in the Expiry Date field.
  4. 4

    Save

    Once everything matches, tap Save (marker 4 in step 2). Your FHV Insurance Certificate step is now complete.

Before you save

  • •Make sure the certificate photo is clear, well-lit, and shows all four corners.
  • •Double-check that the Policy Number you type matches the certificate exactly, including any letters.
  • •Confirm your insurance hasn't expired — you'll need an updated certificate from your insurer first if it has.

Still having trouble? Contact support from within the app for further assistance.
